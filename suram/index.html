About SURAM

SURAM Crafts & Creations is a small, handmade studio born from a love of colour, clay and yarn. From hand-painted festive diyas to crocheted bags and made-to-order surprise gifts, everything is crafted by hand — never mass-produced.

We believe a gift should feel personal. That's why we love making customised pieces: a greeting card in your words, a box that opens into a shower of little surprises, or a craft video keepsake for someone far away.

  • 🌿 Eco-friendly, air-dry clay & natural materials
  • 🎨 Hand-painted, one piece at a time
  • 💝 Made-to-order customisation
