Our Story

A Slice of Incredible India
in the Heart of Norway

Indians in Agder is Arendal's vibrant Indian community group — a warm, inclusive space where culture, tradition and new friendships flourish amidst the Norwegian fjords.

Founded in 2018, we bring together families, students and professionals from across India's rich tapestry of regions, languages and traditions, building a home away from home in Aust-Agder.

Our Mission

To preserve, celebrate and share the rich cultural heritage of India while fostering meaningful connections between the Indian diaspora and the wider Agder community.

🔭

Our Vision

A thriving, united Indian community in Agder where every member feels seen, supported and proud of their dual identity — rooted in India, at home in Norway.

💛

Our Values

Inclusivity, celebration, mutual support and cross-cultural understanding. We believe diversity is Norway's greatest strength.

Governance

Community Rulebook

📋

India i Agder operates on a foundation of mutual respect, inclusion and shared values. These guidelines help us maintain a welcoming community for all.

India i Agder — Community Guidelines

Effective from January 2025 · Arendal, Norway

01
Respect & Inclusion
Every member shall treat all others with dignity and respect, regardless of region, language, religion, age or background. Discrimination of any kind is strictly not tolerated.
02
Membership
Membership is open to all Indians and friends of Indian culture residing in or around Agder. Annual membership fees, if applicable, must be paid by the deadline communicated by the board.
03
Event Conduct
Members attending community events must behave responsibly. Disruptive behaviour, use of offensive language, or actions that bring disrepute to the community will result in removal from the event.
04
Communication
All official communication happens via approved channels (WhatsApp group, email newsletter, website). Spreading misinformation or unverified news through community channels is prohibited.
05
Photography & Media
Photos and videos taken at community events may be used on our website and social media. Members who do not wish to be photographed should notify the organiser. Content involving minors requires explicit parental consent.
06
Volunteering
Members are encouraged to volunteer at community events. Volunteer responsibilities must be fulfilled with commitment. Last-minute cancellations should be communicated as early as possible.
07
Financial Transparency
The treasurer shall maintain clear records of all income and expenditure. An annual financial summary will be shared with all members. Community funds must not be used for personal purposes.
08
Conflict Resolution
Disputes between members should first be resolved informally. If unresolved, the matter should be brought to the board. The board's decision is final and binding.
09
Social Media
Members representing India i Agder on social media must do so positively and professionally. Personal opinions must be clearly stated as such and not presented as official community positions.
10
Amendments
These rules may be amended by a two-thirds majority vote at the Annual General Meeting. Proposed amendments must be submitted to the board at least two weeks before the AGM.

Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

🔒

India i Agder is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Norwegian law.

🗂️

What Data We Collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide: name, email address, and event registrations. We do not collect sensitive personal data. Photos taken at our events may include your image.

🎯

Why We Use It

Your data is used solely to communicate event information, manage membership, and improve community services. We never sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

📅

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you are an active member. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR Article 17.

🍪

Cookies

This website uses only essential functional cookies necessary for the site to operate. We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. No third-party cookies are set without your consent.

✅

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccuracies, request erasure, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority).

📬

Contact & DPO

For any privacy-related requests or questions, contact us at privacy@indiaiagder.no. We aim to respond within 72 hours.
